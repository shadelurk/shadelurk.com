Arcana AutonomaFourteen Races

Steam Early Access — November 17, 2026

Auto-battler card RPG

Twenty years after the War of Unification, Setsuna, daughter of the captain of the sky battleship Bloody Rain, descends to the land below.

Blockades and seizures break out across the nations of fourteen races. Inherit the techniques of those you face as cards, then collect, grow and lead five heroes into battle. The story sets the ship's records beside those of each nation, and the same events are written down differently depending on who kept them.

  • 151 cards from 14 races
  • Ten-card summons and a collection book
  • Enhance and evolve
  • Five-card auto battles
  • Finishers combine into Links
  • Raid bosses with rankings
